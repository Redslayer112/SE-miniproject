# Traffic Management System (Simulation)

**Software Engineering Project · Course: UE24CS341A · PES University**

A simulation-based Traffic Management System designed to model traffic flow, road networks, and traffic signal plans. The project focuses on simulating traffic scenarios, visualizing road networks in 2D, and evaluating traffic performance through metrics and reports.

## Project Overview

The Traffic Management System (Simulation) aims to provide a software-based environment for modelling and analyzing traffic conditions. It supports traffic network representation, signal-plan modelling, simulation execution, and visualization of traffic-related metrics.

The system is designed to help demonstrate traffic behaviour under different configurations and support analysis of traffic flow and signal management strategies.

## Team Responsibilities

## Testing

Testing will be performed according to the Software Test Plan (STP). The focus will include:

- Correctness of traffic simulation behaviour.
- Validation of road network and signal-plan configurations.
- Reliability of the simulation controls and visualization.
- Accuracy of traffic metrics and generated reports.
- Integration between the simulation engine, UI, and reporting components.
